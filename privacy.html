Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

The developer of StudienMap does not collect personal data from users of this app. This policy describes how data is handled within the app.

Data collection

StudienMap does not transmit personal data to the developer or to a developer-operated backend. The app does not include third-party analytics libraries, advertising networks, or tracking.

iCloud and CloudKit

If you use iCloud on your Apple device, your study planning data may be stored and synchronised across your own Apple devices via Apple's iCloud / CloudKit service. This data is processed by Apple under Apple's own terms of service and privacy policy. The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

Study plans can also be shared with selected invited people via iCloud sharing. Shared people can only access the shared planning data. Private notes are not included in shared data.

You can manage or disable iCloud sync for StudienMap at any time through your device's system settings.

Private notes

Private notes you create within StudienMap remain stored locally on your device only. They are not synchronised via iCloud or any other service.

User accounts

StudienMap does not require a user account. No registration, login, or personal identification is needed to use the app.

Device permissions

StudienMap does not request or use access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or health data.

Developer

StudienMap is developed by Dr. Christian Kerstan.
