Frequently asked questions

What is StudienMap?

StudienMap is a study planning app for iPhone and iPad. It helps you structure longer study periods week by week, for example across a full semester or a series of exam periods. The app is intended for individual, self-directed study planning.

Does StudienMap require an account?

No. StudienMap does not require a user account, registration, or login. You can use the app immediately after installation.

Does StudienMap work offline?

Yes. All core planning features work without an internet connection. An internet connection is only needed if you use iCloud sync.

Does StudienMap sync data?

If you use iCloud on your Apple device, StudienMap can synchronise your planning data across your own Apple devices via Apple's iCloud / CloudKit service. You can manage or disable this at any time in your device's system settings.

Are private notes synced?

No. Private notes you create in StudienMap remain stored locally on your device only and are not synchronised via iCloud or any other service.

How can I export my plan?

StudienMap can export a PDF of your planning overview. You can find the export option within the app. The landscape view gives you a compact overview of your full plan, which is also what the PDF reflects.

How can I report a problem?

Please send an email to appsupport@wichtelimwald.de with the following information:

  • iOS version (e.g. iOS 18.0 or later)
  • App version (visible in the App Store or under Settings)
  • Device model (e.g. iPhone 15, iPad Air)
  • A short description of the issue

Including these details helps address your report as efficiently as possible.
